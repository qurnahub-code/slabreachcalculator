About the Tool

The Standard SLA Breach Calculator

Calculating SLA breach penalties manually is a common source of friction between vendors and clients. A Service Level Agreement (SLA) outlines the minimum acceptable standard for services like cloud hosting, SaaS platforms, or managed IT support. When these services fall short—whether through excess downtime, missed response targets, or delayed delivery—the contract typically entitles you to service credits or financial compensation.

This free SLA Breach Calculator is designed to remove the guesswork from enforcing your contracts. By inputting your contracted uptime percentage, actual uptime, and standard penalty structures (such as flat rates or tiered percentage caps), the calculator instantly processes the exact financial overage. Whether you are an IT procurement manager calculating downtime credits against an enterprise AWS/Azure contract, or an operations director validating a managed service provider's monthly report, our tool provides a mathematically sound, defensible figure to attach to your formal claim.

We built this tool to be 100% private and client-side. To learn more about the exact formulas used, check out our guide on how to calculate SLA breach penalties or read our SLA Breach FAQ.
