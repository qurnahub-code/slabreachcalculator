Section 01

Information We Collect

🔒

Short answer: none. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, calculation inputs, or results.

SLABreachCalculator.site does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or submit any form that sends data to a remote server. The calculator fields you fill in exist solely in your browser's memory and are discarded when you close or refresh the page.

Section 02

How Calculations Work

Every calculation performed on this site runs entirely client-side using JavaScript executing in your web browser. Specifically:

  • Input values (uptime percentages, contract values, dates, penalty rates) are read from form fields in your browser.
  • All arithmetic is performed by JavaScript code loaded with the page.
  • Results are written back to the page DOM — also in your browser.
  • No input data or results are sent over the network at any point.

You can verify this independently by opening your browser's developer tools (F12), going to the Network tab, and observing that no outgoing requests carry your form data when you click "Calculate".

Section 03

Cookies & Tracking

This site does not use:

  • Advertising cookies or tracking pixels
  • Analytics cookies (e.g. Google Analytics, Hotjar)
  • Session cookies tied to user identity
  • Local Storage or IndexedDB persistence of your calculation inputs

Google Fonts are loaded from fonts.googleapis.com to render the Inter and IBM Plex Mono typefaces. This request may log your IP address per Google's own privacy policy. If this concerns you, the site remains fully functional with system fonts if you block external font requests.

Section 04

Third-Party Services

We do not integrate any third-party advertising networks, analytics platforms, A/B testing tools, heatmap recorders, or CRM systems.

The only external resource loaded is the Google Fonts stylesheet referenced above. No other third-party JavaScript is loaded on this page.

Section 05

Children's Privacy

This site is a professional business tool intended for adults working in IT management, procurement, and operations. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Section 06

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Because we collect no personal data, no changes to this policy will affect the privacy of previously provided information (there is none to affect).
